How a RoomLattice pilot runs

  1. Import bookings and room types

    You send twelve months of booking exports from your property system. We match rooms, types and cleaning times.

  2. Daily room preparation forecasts

    Each morning at 06:00 the day’s plan arrives, and we measure it against real arrivals.

  3. Weekly operations report

    Managers see rooms ready on time, overtime hours and every plan decision.

Questions

What hotel managers ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

What data do we need?

Twelve months of booking exports from your property system, plus room types and cleaning times.

Does it change our bookings?

No. RoomLattice reads exports and has no write access to your property system.

What does it cost?

A monthly subscription per property. We quote after the pilot.

Does it work for serviced apartments?

Yes. Longer stays and weekly cleans are planned the same way.

Where is our data kept?

In an AWS account of its own, in the region you choose, encrypted at rest and in transit.

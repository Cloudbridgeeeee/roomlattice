# Product

RoomLattice: Prepare hospitality operations for tomorrow’s guests.

AI operational planning software that forecasts guest arrivals, housekeeping workloads and room preparation requirements.

- Founder and CEO: Eniola Adegbite
- Co-founder and CTO: Joseph Ibe
- Industry: Hospitality, Tourism, Machine Learning
- Customers: Hotels, resorts and serviced apartments
- Core features: Occupancy forecasting, housekeeping demand predictions, room readiness planning, service demand analytics, operational scheduling
- MVP: Booking imports and daily room preparation forecasts
- Planned AWS: SageMaker AI, Bedrock, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda
- Business model: Monthly subscriptions per property
- Main action: Request a pilot
